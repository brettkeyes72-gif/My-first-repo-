# Content plan: the leather ski glove cluster

The pillar piece is **Why Leather Still Wins** (built). Each benefit section is already a standalone module, so every spin-off below starts as a deeper version of a module that exists today. Link each spin-off back to the pillar and to the module it grew from.

## Spin-off pieces

| # | Working title | Grows from | Format | Why it earns its place |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How to break in leather gloves | `dexterity` | How-to, 3 min, animated step cards | Highest-intent post-purchase question. Also cuts returns from "they feel stiff." |
| 2 | Leather glove care in 5 minutes | `myths` (care) | Checklist module + short video loop | Evergreen, easy to share, supports durability story. |
| 3 | Is leather waterproof? What to do on wet days | `myths` (waterproof) | Explainer with honest limits, treat/dry/re-treat steps | Builds trust by saying what leather isn't. Strong search demand. |
| 4 | Wind chill and your hands | `wind` | Short explainer, animated wind diagram | Teaches the "why" and sets up insulation/liner content. |
| 5 | Leather vs synthetic: choosing by conditions | whole pillar | Decision guide (cold dry / wet / spring), no spec-sheet claims we can't back | Captures comparison searches. Needs product data before drafting. |
| 6 | What actually keeps hands warm: shell, insulation, liner | `myths` (warm) | Layer diagram, interactive | Fixes the biggest misconception and sells the system, not just the shell. |
| 7 | Cost per day: the math of a glove that lasts | `durability` | Calculator module (price, days per season, seasons) | Interactive, shareable, ties to price objections. Uses our numbers only. |
| 8 | Grip: what to look for in a palm | `grip` | Short guide with palm close-ups | Product education that maps directly to features. |
| 9 | Sizing right for skiing | size finder | Tool + short guide | Links the existing Glove Size Finder; fit drives the dexterity benefit. |
| 10 | Leather for lift days vs touring | `breathe` | Use-case guide | Opens the touring/backcountry audience. |

Suggested order: 1, 2, 3 first (post-purchase, fastest value), then 6, 4, 7, then the rest.

## Reusable modules to add to the library

- **Step cards** (numbered, icon, optional loop): used by 1, 2, 3.
- **Calculator** (inputs plus a live result): used by 7, and later sizing.
- **Layer diagram** (stackable, hover/tap to reveal): used by 6.
- **Callout** (honest limit / pro tip): used everywhere.
- **Product strip** (3 products, linked): end of every piece.
- **Quote/proof** (customer or athlete line): needs real quotes.

## Imagery and motion

- **Hero images** (one per piece): glove in use, shallow depth of field, cold light, leather grain visible. The hero module reads `img/hero-<slug>.jpg`; drop a file in and it appears under the gradient. Needs real TRUCK product photography or a generated set approved by you.
- **Motion** only where it explains: wind blocked, moisture leaving, grip tightening, break-in progress, seasons accumulating. Everything respects reduced-motion.
- **Icons**: one stroke style, inside the cut-corner tile, so ideas read at a glance.

## Open questions before publishing

1. **Copy.** I could only get a summary of the live article, not the exact text. The module copy is drafted from its section outline, so replace it with your original wording or edit it.
2. **Claims.** Keep to what the article supports (wind, grip, breathes better than many synthetic shells, breaks in, lasts seasons). Add numbers only if you have test data.
3. **Links.** `article.json` holds the collection, Snow and size-finder URLs. Confirm them.
4. **Where it lives.** Shopify blog (paste fragments) or a hosted page (use `dist`)?
5. **Photography.** What do we have, and what needs to be shot or generated?
