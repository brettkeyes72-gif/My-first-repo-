Photo checklist

  1. Lay your dominant hand palm up on a table, fingers together and pointing straight up the photo, so your wrist creases show.
  2. Rest the card or coin on your palm, flat, so it's at the same height as your hand. Line the card's long edge up with your middle finger.
  3. Hold the phone flat, straight above your hand at about arm's length (at least 40 cm / 16 in). The frame turns green when the phone is flat, and the photo takes itself.
